# Third-party assets and attribution

This is an independent, unofficial reverse-engineering and reconstruction study of **Subway Surfers 3.61.0**. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SYBO or Kiloo.

The Subway Surfers name, characters, meshes, textures, animation clips, environment artwork, UI art and recovered audio belong to their respective owners. The recovered material in `apps/web/public/recovered/`, its build copies, and the corresponding material inside app downloads are third-party content. This repository does not claim ownership of, or grant a new license to, that material.

App integration, shared gameplay, route assembly, UI, renderer adaptations, asset-conversion scripts and reconstructed effects are described separately from recovered evidence. The project does not claim to reproduce the complete original Unity runtime or Animator.

Replacement character, vehicle, collectible and scenery models were supplied for this reskin. Original attribution and third-party rights continue to apply.

Dependencies retain their own licenses, including Three.js, Vite and TypeScript.

The six Railway World districts were supplied in Railway_World(1).zip. This build adapts them for lane spacing, overhead clearance and browser rendering.

The equipped hoverboard, jetpack and magnet use the user-supplied redesigns. The night railway music and Venom drop collection cue in apps/web/public/audio were synthesized for this build.
